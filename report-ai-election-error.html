    About this project

    Why it exists

    Most worry about AI and elections is about bad actors: deepfakes, fake robocalls, influence campaigns. This project looks at something quieter. Ordinary AI tools, used the ordinary way, sometimes get election facts wrong. Outdated training data, a bad source or a made-up detail is enough.

    Test labs can show what a model might do. Only real reports show what it actually told real voters. That is what we collect.

    Who runs it

    AI Election Errors is run by Hoplight, an AI strategy firm. Hoplight covers the cost. No party, candidate, campaign or AI company funds it or has a say in it. We treat every AI tool the same way.

    If you build one of these tools

    You can respond to any report or finding, or flag a mistake in our work: whit@hoplight.ai.

    How we weigh reports

    We don't fact-check each report before storing it. Anything we publish is reviewed first, and stronger evidence counts for more:

    • AA share link to the conversation that anyone can check
    • BA screenshot, and we can follow up with you
    • CA screenshot, reported anonymously
    • DPasted text only

    What we keep

    Anonymous: what you type and upload, and the state your connection came from. We don't link your reports together.

    With contact info: the same, plus your city and browser type, and your contact details, stored separately and seen only by our review team. We never publish, sell or share them, and we ask before connecting you with anyone.

    Our records never include your internet address. Photos lose their hidden location and camera data before they leave your device.
